Privacy policy

The short version: the WhoSees app sends nothing anywhere, and this website counts visits privately, without cookies.

Last updated 2026-11-14

The WhoSees app

WhoSees does not collect, send or sell any of your information. Everything it shows is worked out on your own device, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • What it looks at: while a live view is running, the details of your device's network connections: which app made them, the address and port they go to, the website name when it's visible, and how much data was sent. For data sent without encryption, it checks the parts needed to say what kind of data it was (for example a location). It does not read the contents of encrypted traffic.
  • Where that goes: nowhere. It's shown on your screen and discarded when you press Stop or close WhoSees.
  • What it saves: only your settings (such as your home city and display preferences), stored on your device.
  • Lists it uses: the IP-location, tracker and threat lists are built into the app and used offline. WhoSees doesn't look anything up online.
  • Links: the website, source code and "buy me a coffee" links open in your browser only when you click them.

Windows and Linux

Deep mode asks your system for administrator permission so WhoSees can read where network traffic goes. Your password goes to your system's own security prompt; WhoSees never sees or stores it.

The whosees.app website

  • Visit counting: we use Umami, an open-source analytics tool running on our own server. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't store your IP address. It records the page you viewed, the site that linked you here, your browser, operating system, device type and approximate country, plus clicks on the download and "buy me a coffee" buttons. We use this only to see how many people visit and download WhoSees.
  • Delivery: the site is delivered through Cloudflare, which, like any network, sees your IP address to send you the page.
  • Downloads come from GitHub, and tips go through PayPal. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Children

WhoSees doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because WhoSees is open source, you can always check what the app does in its source code.
